Account & Data Deletion Request

App: Easy Attendance  |  Developer: ISTECH

This page explains how to request deletion of your Easy Attendance account and associated data.

How to Request Deletion

  • Send an email to jspark5938@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in to the App.
  • Subject: [Easy Attendance] Account Deletion Request
  • Include the Google account email address you used in the App.
After verifying your identity, we will complete the deletion within 7 days and notify you by email.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account information (name, email address, profile photo)
  • All attendance data stored in the cloud, including groups, student information, and attendance records

Data That Will Be Retained

No personal information is retained after account deletion, except where retention is required by law, in which case it is destroyed after the required period.

Deleting Data Without Deleting Your Account

Email us at the address above and state that it is a data deletion request. We will delete your attendance data while keeping your account.
